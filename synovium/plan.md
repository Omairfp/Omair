## Facts used

| # | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Synovium is the soft tissue lining diarthrodial joints, tendon sheaths and bursae. | S1 |
| 2 | It lines the joint capsule from inside; articular cartilage is not covered by it. | S2, S3 |
| 3 | Two layers: intima (surface cell layer) and subintima (underlying tissue). | S1 |
| 4 | Intima is 1-2 cells thick (often 2-3 in areolar synovium), about 20-40 µm; areolar subintima up to 5 mm. | S1 (the PMC text prints "20-40mm": a lost µ symbol, since 1-3 cells cannot be 20-40 mm) |
| 5 | Areolar synovium is often crimped into folds and may carry villi. Normal synovium at arthroscopy looks bland, without villi, granularity or increased vascularity. | S1, S7 |
| 6 | Type A synoviocytes: macrophage lineage (CD68+, CD163+), a minority of the normal intima. | S1 |
| 7 | Type B synoviocytes: fibroblast lineage (CD55+), the majority; they make hyaluronan; lubricin (PRG4) sits at the synovial surface. | S1, S2 |
| 8 | No basement membrane under the intima. | S1 |
| 9 | Capillaries lie just below or within the intima; some are fenestrated, fenestrae facing the surface. Larger venules, arterioles and lymphatics lie deeper. | S1 |
| 10 | Subintima holds fibroblasts, fat cells, vessels, collagen; a type I collagen-rich band under the intima forms the physical membrane. Three types: areolar, adipose, fibrous. | S1 |
| 11 | Synovial fluid is rich in hyaluronan (about 2-4 mg/mL); lubricin gives boundary lubrication. | S1, S6 |
| 12 | Functions: non-adherent surface, lubrication, control of fluid volume and composition, nutrition of chondrocytes. | S1 |

Not sourced, so not stated in the film: an exact colour of living synovium, an exact fluid volume (sources range
0.5 to 4 mL), an exact type A percentage.

## Style card

| Line | Value |
|---|---|
| Camera | Low three-quarter, 25-35 degrees above the surface, fov 35; subject fills 60-70% of the frame |
| Palette | H&E-led: matrix pink `#F2B8D0`, collagen rose `#E58FB4`, type B cell blue `#5F7FE0`, type A cell purple `#8B3FB8`, capillary red `#D8342F`, venule `#7A3CA0`, lymphatic `#9BC46A`, fat `#F6E7B0`, cartilage `#DCEBF5`, bone `#E9DDC6`, background navy `#0B1020` **(choice: dark background for contrast; slides are white)** |
| Light | Soft studio key from upper left, low contrast, soft contact shadows |
| Materials | Wet, slightly glossy tissue (roughness 0.35-0.5); matrix translucent so vessels read through it |
| Density | Intima packed 2 cells deep over the whole surface; subintima loose, with fibres, vessels and fat |
| Shapes | Rounded, organic; no hard edges |
| Motion | Hyaluronan strands drift from the intima into the cavity; filtrate dots rise from capillaries |

Scale in the tissue block: 1 unit = 100 µm. Block 300 µm wide, intima 20-35 µm, capillary 7 µm diameter,
fat cells 50-60 µm. The joint in shots 1-2 is a schematic of a hinge joint **(choice)**, not a scan of a knee.
